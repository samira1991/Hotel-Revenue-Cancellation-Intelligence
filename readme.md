# Hotel Revenue & Cancellation Intelligence

Hotel Revenue & Cancellation Intelligence is a data analytics and business intelligence project focused on analyzing hotel booking behavior, cancellation patterns, revenue performance, customer segmentation, and seasonal demand trends. The project uses SQL, BigQuery, and Tableau to transform raw hotel booking data into actionable business insights that support better revenue management and operational decision-making.

The analysis investigates the main factors associated with booking cancellations, including lead time, customer type, deposit policies, and market segments. It also evaluates the estimated financial impact of canceled reservations by measuring potential lost revenue and cancellation rates across different hotel categories.

In addition, the project explores:

* Revenue performance by hotel type
* Customer and market profitability
* Seasonal booking trends and demand fluctuations
* Booking behavior patterns
* Financial risks caused by cancellations

The goal of this project is to help hotel managers and decision-makers:

* Reduce potential revenue loss
* Improve cancellation management strategies
* Optimize pricing and occupancy planning
* Identify the most valuable customer segments
* Better understand seasonal demand behavior
